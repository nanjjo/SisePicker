# Third-party components

SisePicker bundles Python, Tcl/Tk, RapidOCR/ONNX Runtime, OpenCV, Pillow, python-pptx and their dependencies. Exact dependency pins are in requirements-build.txt. The release ZIP includes license/notice files discovered in installed distributions under THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES, with INDEX.json recording package versions and available metadata. Build tools may also appear in that inventory. Package-specific and model-specific terms remain applicable; this inventory does not relicense them.

Python: https://www.python.org/psf/license/ ; Tcl/Tk: https://www.tcl-lang.org/software/tcltk/license.html ; RapidOCR and model provenance: https://github.com/RapidAI/RapidOCR . Optional Korean model download locations are documented in download_korean_model.py. Site content belongs to its respective owners and is not distributed as a training dataset. Two minimal public-page regression fixtures are included only to reproduce parsing and OCR edge cases; rights remain with the respective owners.

No open-source license has been selected for SisePicker itself. Public source availability does not grant additional license rights.
